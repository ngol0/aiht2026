AIHT 2026

Symposium on AI in Health Technologies · Aalto University and ELLIS Institute Finland · Espoo, Finland, December 2026

Call for papers

AIHT 2026 is the research symposium of the AI in Health Technologies programme at Aalto University and ELLIS Institute Finland. It invites short papers reporting original student research on machine learning for health and biomedical technologies, and runs a full single-blind peer review process in which every participant serves as a reviewer.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Medical image analysis: segmentation, registration, reconstruction, detection
  • Generative models for biomedical data
  • Clinical language models and multimodal clinical records
  • Physiological signals, wearables and sensor data
  • Predictive modelling and clinical decision support
  • Uncertainty, robustness, fairness and evaluation in health AI

Papers report completed work with experimental results. Two independent papers on the same problem are welcome.

Important dates

Paper submission6 December 2026

Submission

Papers are submitted through the symposium's Microsoft CMT site: submission site. Authors register with their Aalto email address. One submission per author group.

  • Six to eight pages, excluding references
  • Springer LNCS format, submitted as PDF
  • Author names and affiliations included; review is single-blind

Submissions outside the page limit or not in the required format are subject to deduction.

Review process

Each paper receives two to three independent reviews. Review is single-blind: reviewers see the author names, authors do not see reviewer identities. There is no rebuttal phase, and reviews are final.

Each review consists of a summary of the work, strengths and weaknesses supported by specific evidence from the paper, questions to the authors, an overall score, and a self-assessed reviewer confidence. Scoring criteria are stated in the review form and in the reviewer guidelines issued to the programme committee.
